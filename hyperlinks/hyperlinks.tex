\documentclass[11pt]{article}

\usepackage{hyperref}

\begin{document}

\centerline{\Large\bf Hyperlinks Example}\par
\bigskip

\section{External Links}

Link with custom text: \href{https://www.google.com}{Google}.

Bare URL displayed as-is: \url{https://www.archlinux.org}.

Email link: \href{mailto:example@example.com}{example@example.com}.

\section{Internal Links}

Jump to Section \ref{sec:target} using \texttt{\textbackslash ref}.

You can also use \hyperref[sec:target]{a custom label} with \texttt{\textbackslash hyperref}.

\section{Target Section}
\label{sec:target}

This is the section linked to above.
