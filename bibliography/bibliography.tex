\section{Inline Citations}

Use \texttt{\textbackslash parencite} for a parenthetical citation: \parencite{knuth1984}.

Use \texttt{\textbackslash textcite} when the author is part of the sentence:
\textcite{lamport1994} introduced a document preparation system widely used today.

You can also cite online sources \parencite{wikipedia-latex} or books \parencite{goossens1993}.

Multiple citations can be grouped: \parencite{knuth1984,lamport1994}.
