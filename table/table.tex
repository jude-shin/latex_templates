\section{Basic Booktabs Table}

Use \texttt{toprule}, \texttt{midrule}, and \texttt{bottomrule} from the \texttt{booktabs} package for clean, professional tables.

\begin{table}[h!]
    \centering
    \caption{Sample data table}
    \label{tab:sample}
    \begin{tabular}{lcc}
        \toprule
        Name   & Value & Unit  \\
        \midrule
        Alpha  & 1.23  & m/s   \\
        Beta   & 4.56  & m/s   \\
        Gamma  & 7.89  & m/s   \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Table \ref{tab:sample} shows how to reference a table by its label.

\section{Multi-column Header}

Use \texttt{\textbackslash multicolumn} to span columns.

\begin{table}[h!]
    \centering
    \caption{Multi-column header example}
    \begin{tabular}{lcccc}
        \toprule
        & \multicolumn{2}{c}{Group A} & \multicolumn{2}{c}{Group B} \\
        \cmidrule(lr){2-3} \cmidrule(lr){4-5}
        Item & Min & Max & Min & Max \\
        \midrule
        X & 1 & 5 & 2 & 6 \\
        Y & 3 & 7 & 4 & 8 \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
